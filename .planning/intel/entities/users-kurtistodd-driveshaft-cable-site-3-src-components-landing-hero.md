# Hero.jsx

## Purpose

Renders the hero section of the landing page with product branding, tagline, call-to-action buttons, and quick product specifications. Features industrial-themed styling with gradient backgrounds, caution stripe accents, and grid overlays.

## Exports

- `Hero` (default): Main hero component displaying the DRIVESHAFT CABLE branding, product description, CTA buttons (Request Quote, Learn More), and quick stats (5/32" cable, 39" length, industrial-grade certification). Includes product image with magnification on hover.
